
# Employee Attrition & HR Analytics Dashboard
[https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/5de2cdf5-741b-49c5-a30b-ef1b9a0f2e8e/9697ff9ac56fb53fc6e2?experience=power-bi]

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee attrition and identify the key factors associated with employee turnover.

##  Business Problem

A company is experiencing employee attrition and wants to understand:

- How many employees are leaving the organization?
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles have higher attrition?
- Is employee attrition associated with overtime and burnout?
- Does employee engagement and work-life balance relate to attrition?
- Is compensation and career progression associated with employee turnover?
- Is perceived AI-related job risk associated with higher attrition?

The goal is to turn employee data into actionable HR insights.

## Project Objective

To analyze employee attrition and identify the key factors associated with employee turnover, with a focus on:

- Compensation
- Career progression
- Workload
- Employee wellbeing
- Work environment
- AI-related job concerns

The insights can help HR teams identify areas that may require attention and support better employee retention strategies.

##  Key Insights

- **5,000 employees** analyzed with an overall attrition rate of **17.90%**.
- **Customer Support** has the highest department-level attrition at **24.06%**.
- **High Burnout:** 52.22% attrition.
- **High Overtime:** 51.24% attrition.
- **Low Engagement:** 51.13% attrition.
- **Low Work-Life Balance:** 40.71% attrition.
- **Low Salary Hike:** 31.79% attrition.
- **8+ Years Promotion Gap:** ~33% attrition.
- **High AI Job Risk:** 24.11% attrition.


## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, HR teams could consider:

- Focus on reducing **employee burnout and excessive overtime**.
- Improve **employee engagement and work-life balance**.
- Review **salary hikes and long promotion gaps**.
- Focus retention efforts on **high-attrition departments and job roles**.
- Address employee concerns related to **AI job risk** through communication and upskilling.

> These findings indicate associations within the dataset and should not be interpreted as direct causation.
